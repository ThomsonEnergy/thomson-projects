Location Tracking Consent

Please read the Notice of Workplace Surveillance and Computer Surveillance Policy first.

This form is optional. If you do not sign it, you clock in and out by hand with a note instead, and nothing changes for your job or pay.

If you sign, I agree that the TEops iPhone app may record my location:
- Once at the moment I clock in, and once when I clock out, to check it against the job site.
- During an "On my way" trip that I start myself, until I arrive, so the customer can be told when I will get there.

I understand that:
- My location is never recorded when I am clocked off.
- The app shows a clear indicator whenever my location is being recorded.
- I will also be asked by my iPhone to allow location for TEops. I can turn it off in my iPhone settings at any time.
- Location records are kept with my timesheet records.
- I can withdraw this consent at any time in TEops or by telling a director, and it stops straight away.

By signing, I consent to location tracking as described above.

Thomson Energy Referral Rewards (Clients)

Draft for the owner's read. The amounts below are today's settings; the live document the client receives prints the amounts from TEops Settings on the day it is sent, so this file is the wording, not the figures.

Purpose
Thomson Energy rewards clients who send us work. When you pass on someone who needs solar, a battery, a switchboard upgrade, a fault fixed or any other electrical work, and we win that job, you get a payment or a credit. This document explains how it works.

1. Who it is for
* Clients Thomson Energy has turned the Referral Rewards option on for. You will know because you have received your personal referral link.
* The link is yours. Anyone who sends a job through it earns the reward for your account, so share it with your staff and your contacts as you like.

2. How to refer a job
* Open your link. It works on any phone with no login.
* Choose Someone else, then choose one of two kinds of referral:
  - Quick referral: their name, their phone number and a line about what they need. About 30 seconds. Use it for a business contact or a name passed on in conversation.
  - Full referral: the questions for that kind of job and a few photos (the roof, the switchboard, the fault). About a minute on site. This earns the higher reward because it saves us a site visit.
* Tick the box to say you have told the person Thomson Energy will contact them. We tell them on first contact that you passed on their details, as the Privacy Act asks us to.
* The same link also lets you ask for a quote for your own job (choose Me). That is not a referral and earns no reward.

3. What you earn
* A full referral earns, by the size of the job we win (the total the customer pays, including GST, after any solar or battery rebates):
  - under $1,000: nothing
  - $1,000 to $4,999: $80
  - $5,000 to $14,999: $200
  - $15,000 and over: $500
* A quick referral earns a flat $50 when the job we win is $1,000 or more.
* One reward per job. If the same person is referred twice, the first referral counts.

4. When you earn it
* The reward is earned the day the customer approves our quote, and becomes payable once they have paid their deposit (or their first invoice, when the job has no deposit). Nothing is earned for a quote that is declined or expires, or for a job that falls over before the deposit is paid.
* You can see every referral and its progress in your Thomson Energy client portal: Received, Being quoted, Quoted, Won, Credit due, Credited or Paid, or Not won.

5. How you receive it
* As a credit: the amount is taken off your next Thomson Energy invoice as its own line. This is the usual way.
* As a payment: paid to your nominated bank account, if Thomson Energy has set your account up that way. A business that is registered for GST sends us a tax invoice for the amount; a credit needs nothing.
* You never see the referred customer's quote or price. Their job is theirs.

6. What does not earn a reward
* Your own jobs, or a job at an address where you already have a quote or job with us.
* A person who is already a Thomson Energy client with an open quote or job for that work.
* A referral made by a Thomson Energy employee.
* Rewards are paid to the client's account only, never to an individual who works for the client. If your staff send us work through your link, the reward is yours.

7. Changes
* Thomson Energy can change the amounts, the tiers or this scheme, or withdraw it, at any time. A referral already won is paid at the amounts that applied on the day the quote was approved.

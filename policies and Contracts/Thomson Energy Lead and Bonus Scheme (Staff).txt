Thomson Energy Lead and Bonus Scheme (Staff)

Draft for the owner's read. The amounts below are today's settings; the live document each employee signs prints the amounts from TEops Settings on the day it is issued, so this file is the wording, not the figures.

Purpose
Thomson Energy pays a bonus to employees who bring in work and who quote jobs that finish under budget. This document explains what counts, how much it pays and how it is paid.

1. Your referral link
* Every employee has a personal referral link in TEops (Home > My link). It opens a short form on any phone with no login.
* Give it to anyone who asks about solar, a battery, a switchboard, a fault or any electrical work: a mate, a neighbour, a customer's friend. They fill it in themselves and it arrives in TEops as your lead.

2. Leads you fill in on site
* When a customer asks about more work while you are on a job, use New lead on My Day. Tick Same customer as the job I'm on and the details fill themselves; add photos of the roof, the switchboard or the fault.
* These count on your KPIs (leads sent, leads that became quotes, leads won and their value). They do not pay the link bonus, because filling the form in on site is part of your paid work.

3. The link bonus
* $50 for every job we win from a lead that came through your link, earned the day the customer approves the quote and payable once they have paid their deposit (or their first invoice, when the job has no deposit). Nothing is earned for a quote that is declined or expires, for a job that falls over before the deposit is paid, or for a job under $1,000.
* One bonus per job. A lead that is already a Thomson Energy client with an open quote or job for that work, or your own household's job, does not count.

4. The under budget bonus
* If you quoted a job (you are shown as Quoted by on the quote) and the job finishes under budget, you earn a bonus: today a flat $100 (the owner may change this to a share of the saving, with a cap; the current rule is shown in TEops Settings and on your staff profile).
* It is paid once the job is Complete and no defect or warranty job has been raised against it in the first 30 days.
* Under budget means: when the job is marked Complete, what it cost Thomson Energy (labour at cost plus every material and purchase order on the job) is at or below what the quote allowed for (the labour and material costs the quote was priced on, plus any approved variations). TEops works this out by itself; nobody adds it up by hand.
* A job that runs over budget earns nothing and costs you nothing.

5. How it is paid
* Bonuses are added to your pay as a Lead bonus line in the pay run after they become payable. They are taxed like any other pay, and superannuation applies as the law requires.
* Your TEops staff profile shows every lead, every bonus earned and when it was paid.

6. The rules
* Never promise a customer a price, a date or a rebate to get a lead. Say Thomson Energy will contact them.
* Tell the person you are passing their details on. We tell them again on first contact, as the Privacy Act asks us to.
* A lead entered under someone else's name, or a referral of work that was already coming to Thomson Energy, is not paid and may be treated as misconduct under the Code of Conduct.
* This scheme is discretionary. It is not a term of your employment contract and it sits on top of your award and contract entitlements. Thomson Energy can change the amounts, change the rules or withdraw the scheme at any time, without notice, for any lead or job not yet won. A bonus already earned is still paid, at the amount that applied on the day it was earned.
* A bonus is paid through payroll like any other pay: tax is withheld and superannuation applies.
* Thomson Energy pays bonuses to its own employees only. If you are referred work by someone who works for one of our clients, the reward goes to that client's account under the client scheme, never to the person privately.

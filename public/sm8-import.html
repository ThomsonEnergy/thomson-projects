ServiceM8 Import

Brings the jobs still open in ServiceM8 (status Work Order) across as Direct Jobs. Loading the list is a dry run: it only reads from ServiceM8 and saves nothing. Check each job, untick any you would rather finish in ServiceM8, then press Import. Jobs already brought across are skipped, so this can be run again on cutover day.

Review

All amounts are ex GST, like everywhere else in the app. Each job comes across with its ServiceM8 line items: every bundle as a prebuild with its real parts and labour, at the prices the client was quoted, so labour and materials split properly when it is invoiced to Xero. Lettered jobs (4515A deposit, 4515B progress claim, and so on) are not imported as jobs: a Completed one counts toward 4515's invoiced to date, and the app only lets you invoice what is left. Check the Labour/Material choice on any line that is not part of a prebuild.

Docs and photos

Once jobs are imported, this copies their ServiceM8 attachments across: pictures into the job's Photos, everything else (PDFs, plans, HEIC photos, videos...) into its Documents under a "ServiceM8" folder. It runs in the background, so you can leave this page, and it is safe to press again: copied files are skipped, failed ones are retried, and anything left over when a run hits its 15 minute limit is picked up next time.

ServiceM8 Import

Moves everything out of ServiceM8 so it can be cancelled. Every client comes across. A ServiceM8 Quote becomes a quote, a Work Order becomes a job, and a Completed job becomes an Archived job under its old number (ServiceM8 #4809 is J4809) with its photos, documents, invoices, time and notes. Jobs marked Unsuccessful stay behind. Nothing is sent to anyone and nothing goes to Xero. It only reads from ServiceM8.

Dry run reads ServiceM8 and shows the numbers. It adds no clients, jobs or quotes.

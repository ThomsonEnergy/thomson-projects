# TEops user-friendliness review (19 Oct 2026)

Read-only review of every page in `public/` for things that make the app harder to use than it needs to be. Nothing here is built. The owner picks items, gets a plan, says yes, then they are built in one delivery. Security and data bugs were handled separately in `docs/BUG_REVIEW.md`.

**How it was done:** 18 reviewers (12 by page area, 6 by lens: phone first, feedback and saving, consistency, navigation, readability, speed), each list checked by an accuracy skeptic (reads the cited code, drops anything that is not there or already exists) and an owner skeptic (drops anything a 10 person trade business would not notice). A critic then found four thin areas (job invoicing and Xero, finishing a job on site, login and policy update, STCs / price lists / assets / public pages) and those were reviewed the same way. Every item cites the file and lines it came from.

**Picking:** quote the item number (for example "UX 3, 7 and 24"). Impact is high (daily pain or blocks work), medium or low. Effort is small (under an hour), medium (a few hours) or large (a day or more, or needs a migration).

**Do first (top ten, best first):**
1. Clock out and clock in modals: disable the button and show the overlay first
2. My Day shows Clock in before it knows you are clocked in
3. Inputs are 14px so iPhones zoom on every tap
4. Save buttons stay live while saving, double taps make duplicates
5. CCEW and Installed equipment cards are hidden on the day the job is finished
6. Invoice and job dates default to the UTC day, so morning work is dated yesterday
7. Every page blocks on an unpinned CDN copy of supabase-js
8. Job tabs: not in the URL, office tabs first, strip scrolls off the phone
9. Site address and client phone never shown on the job page
10. Buttons shrunk below 40px across the app

**Quick wins:** 128 of the items below are small effort (under an hour each); each item shows its own effort, so pick by theme.

### Clock in, My Day, schedule, timesheets and tasks
The field crew's daily path: make the clock buttons respond instantly, never show the wrong state, replace popup chains with real buttons, and let people find today and their own row.
- **1. Clock out and clock in modals: disable the button and show the overlay first** (public/js/supabase-client.js 2543-2595, 3239-3243; public/my-day.html 146; public/home.html 155; impact high, effort small; helps field staff). openClockOutModal runs three or four queries before anything appears and the caller never disables the button, so on poor signal a second tap stacks two overlays. openJobClockInModal has the same shape. Upgrade: Set btn.disabled = true on tap, append the overlay straight away with a Loading... line (the openEditQuickNavPanel pattern at home.html 294-297), then run the queries and renderStep1(). Re-enable in onDone or on error. Same in openJobClockInModal.
- **2. Clock in on My Day can be submitted twice** (public/my-day.html 443-489; impact high, effort small; helps field staff). The job branch inserts the time_entries row at 480 without disabling the button; the TAFE branch above does. A second tap creates a second open entry that later shows as a timesheet overlap on Home. Upgrade: btn.disabled = true and 'Clocking in...' before the insert, restore on error, keep disabled until checkActiveClock swaps the view. Same on the switch-job and clock-out save buttons. No optimistic swap.
- **3. My Day shows Clock in before it knows you are clocked in** (public/my-day.html 43-70, 127-152, 574-586; impact high, effort small; helps field staff). #clock-idle is visible by default and checkActiveClock runs last after role, today's schedule and my schedule, so a clocked-in worker sees Clock in for seconds; a failed query silently falls through to the idle card. Upgrade: Hide both blocks behind 'Checking your clock...', run checkActiveClock first, then loadTodaysSchedule, loadMySchedule and loadTasks in one Promise.all. On error show an error-box 'Could not check your clock, check your signal' with Retry instead of the idle card.
- **4. Tasks page Done is instant and tiny** (public/tasks.html 75-78, 106-115; impact high, effort small; helps everyone). Done and Pick up are 29px buttons 6px apart, and Done updates immediately with no disabled state; the task vanishes and this page has no completed list. Upgrade: Disable the button on tap, drop the inline font-size and padding so they get the default size (the phone min-height rule covers the rest).
- **5. Tapping a schedule booking fires a chain of confirm() popups** (public/schedule.html 460-489, 492-524, 730-756, 941-985; impact high, effort medium; helps everyone). Tapping a block asks 'Clock in? (Cancel to remove)' then 'Remove this block?', so Cancel means delete; all seven removal paths delete with no undo and no error, while drag-to-bin already has an Undo toast via deleteBooking(). Upgrade: Replace the confirm chain on the phone list with a small poOverlay card: job title, then Clock in, Open job, Remove booking (edit roles only), Close. Route all seven removals through deleteBooking() so the undo toast and error handling apply. Leave the other confirms.
- **6. Field staff cannot report an incident from site** (public/incidents.html 96-99; public/incident.html 692; public/js/hr-shared.js 395-472; public/js/supabase-client.js 1831; supabase/migrations baseline 4131-4134; impact high, effort large; helps field staff). Every path to create an incident is admin/finance only (nav, pages, hrNewIncident and the RLS policy), so a near miss means ringing the office and photos are not captured on the spot. Upgrade: One 'Report an incident' button on My Day: what happened, photos via compressImage, job prefilled from the current clock-in, time defaulting to now. Security-definer RPC (report_incident) plus an insert path on the staff-files bucket, no change to who can read incidents. Needs a migration.
- **7. Single-stage jobs still demand a stage tap before Clock in** (public/my-day.html 386-419, 462-466; impact medium, effort small; helps field staff). Stage chips render with nothing selected and Clock in refuses with 'Pick at least one stage' even when there is one stage; every Quick Job has exactly one. Upgrade: In loadCostCentreChips, when centres.length === 1 push that id into selectedClockCostCentres and add the selected class, matching supabase-client.js 3241.
- **8. Home 'Switch job' is a link to another page, not the modal** (public/home.html 143-158; public/js/supabase-client.js 3076; impact medium, effort small; helps field staff). The link loads My Day, waits for its queries, then the worker finds Switch job there. openJobSwitchModal already exists and refreshClockButton fetches every field it needs. Upgrade: Replace the link with a secondary button beside Clock out calling openJobSwitchModal(data, refreshClockButton).
- **9. My Day task buttons are 28px tall and errors are ignored** (public/my-day.html 506-509, 546-559; impact medium, effort small; helps field staff). 'I can do this' and 'Done' are 12px with 6px padding, side by side, and a failed Done update just re-renders the old list with no message. Upgrade: Drop the inline sizing so they use the base button, min-height 44px on phones and put them under the task text. Show error.message in an error-box in the card when the update fails. No undo, no timers.
- **10. Clock-out and task photo inputs force the camera** (public/js/supabase-client.js 2979; public/project.html 5046; impact medium, effort small; helps field staff). capture="environment" skips the Photo Library option and ignores multiple on iPhone, so photos taken earlier in the day cannot be attached at clock-out. Upgrade: Remove capture="environment" from the clock-out input and the task photo input so iOS offers Take Photo or Photo Library and honours multiple.
- **11. Schedule has no loading state and hides fetch errors** (public/schedule.html 236-275, 299-317, 408; impact medium, effort small; helps everyone). The old day's bookings sit under the new date label until three queries return, Prev/Next stay live, and a failed fetch renders an empty day that reads as nobody booked. Upgrade: Add an .is-loading class (dim, no pointer events) to the card and disable Prev/Next/Today until the render finishes; destructure error from each query and show an error-box with Retry on failure instead of an empty day.
- **12. Staff rows in random order, viewer not first** (public/schedule.html 365-368, 422-424, 1042-1045; impact medium, effort small; helps field staff). loadPickerData has no .order(), so a worker scrolls through colleagues in a different order each load to find their own block. - **13. How's the job? step books weekends and cannot be skipped** (public/js/supabase-client.js 2900-2954; impact medium, effort medium; helps field staff). Only 'Complete' and 'Not complete, back tomorrow', no neutral option, and 'Not complete' books the next calendar day so a Friday clock-out books Saturday. The double-booking warning auto-advances after 2.5s. Upgrade: Roll Saturday and Sunday to Monday and print the date on the button ('Not complete, back Mon 20 Oct'). Give the double-booking warning its own Continue button. Add a quiet text link under the buttons: 'Not finished, do not book me'.
- **14. Pipeline move reloads the page and dumps you at column one** (public/dashboard.html 165-184; impact medium, effort medium; helps office). Changing a card's stage runs location.reload(), losing about 3,000px of sideways scroll; errors go to alert(). Upgrade: On stage change move the card's node into the new column, update both count badges, replace alert() with an error line in the card. No undo toast.
- **15. Tasks page cannot edit or delete a task; due dates raw** (public/tasks.html 58-80, 68, 203-224; impact medium, effort medium; helps everyone). Only Done and Pick up exist, so a typo or wrong assignee can only be cleared by marking it done; due dates print as 2026-10-19 with no overdue flag. Upgrade: Format due dates en-AU, show Overdue in red, sort by due date. Add edit and delete by reopening openAddTaskPanel through its prefill path with Save and Delete; if that is not a clean reuse, add Delete only.
- **16. Daily quote inside the H1 pushes the clock button down** (public/home.html 71, 890-899; impact low, effort small; helps everyone). Long quotes wrap the 22px heading to four or five lines on a phone, dropping the Clock button and tiles below the fold. Upgrade: Keep the h1 as 'Welcome back, Jasper' and put the quote in a small subtitle line under the tiles.
- **17. Break step forces a blank time picker** (public/js/supabase-client.js 2754-2786, 2591; impact low, effort small; helps field staff). state.breakStart starts empty and Continue refuses until a time is picked, so every day over 6 hours means the scroll-wheel picker. Upgrade: Default state.breakStart to 12:00 so Yes then Continue works. No quick chips.
- **18. Schedule hides what it does and shows controls Staff cannot use** (public/schedule.html 160, 185-192, 332-350, 621, 662-674; impact low, effort small; helps field staff). Staff on a laptop see the drag sidebar although drops do nothing, double-click to add office time is undocumented, and opening a past day silently deletes unclocked bookings. Upgrade: Hide .day-board-sidebar and the drag hint when !canEditSchedule() with a 'View only' subtitle; one hint line about double-clicking an empty slot; one line above the board on past days saying how many bookings were removed because they were not clocked.
- **19. Timesheets: no This week button** (public/timesheets.html 40-42, 284-285; impact low, effort small; helps everyone). Week navigation is Previous/Next only, so getting back to the current pay week means counting taps. Upgrade: Add a This week button between Previous and Next that resets weekStart to getPayWeekStart(new Date()).

### Finishing a job on site: CCEW and installed equipment
The day the job is done: show the CCEW and equipment cards when the electrician actually has the certificate and serials, make one store the truth, and take the office jargon out of the field steps.

The default handover task points at a 'customer asset list' that is not on the page. Upgrade: Show both cards for any job with a job_number, no stage check, with the CCEW card on the Summary tab next to Installed equipment. Change the default task text to 'Record the installed equipment under Installed equipment on the Summary tab'. If a nudge is wanted, one 'No CCEW uploaded' line in the existing Needs attention card; no header badge, no button on the task row.
- **21. CCEW upload and the handover checklist look in two different places** (public/project.html 2784-2785, 3542, 5681-5683; netlify/functions/_shared/solar-compliance-rules.js 67-71; netlify/functions/check-solar-compliance.js 33, 58; impact high, effort small; helps everyone).
